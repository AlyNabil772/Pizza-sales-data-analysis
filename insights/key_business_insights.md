# 📌 Key Business Insights – Pizza Sales Analysis

## 1️⃣ Overall Sales Performance
- Total revenue reached **$817,860** from **21,350 orders**.
- A total of **49,574 pizzas** were sold.
- The average order value is **$38**, with an average of **2.32 pizzas per order**.

📌 **Business Insight:**  
The business demonstrates strong order value and effective cross-selling, with customers typically purchasing more than two pizzas per order.

---

## 2️⃣ Sales by Pizza Category
- Classic pizzas generate the highest revenue among all categories.
- Supreme and Chicken categories follow closely in total sales.
- Veggie pizzas contribute the lowest share of total revenue.

📌 **Business Insight:**  
Classic pizzas should remain the core product offering, while targeted promotions could be used to boost demand for lower-performing categories.

---

## 3️⃣ Sales by Pizza Size
- Large and Medium pizzas account for the majority of total revenue.
- Small and XL sizes contribute significantly less to overall sales.

📌 **Business Insight:**  
Upselling Medium and Large sizes should be a key sales strategy, as they drive the highest revenue contribution.

---

## 4️⃣ Order Trends by Day
- Orders peak on **Fridays and Thursdays**, indicating strong end-of-week demand.
- Lower order volumes are observed earlier in the week.

📌 **Business Insight:**  
Targeted promotions or bundles earlier in the week could help balance demand and improve overall weekly performance.

---

## 5️⃣ Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales show noticeable peaks during mid-year months.
- A gradual decline appears toward the end of the year.

📌 **Business Insight:**  
Seasonal promotions and limited-time offers can help sustain demand during lower-performing months.

---

## 6️⃣ Strategic Recommendations
- Focus on Classic pizza offerings and high-performing sizes.
- Leverage peak demand days for premium pricing or bundled offers.
- Introduce promotions to stimulate demand during off-peak periods.
